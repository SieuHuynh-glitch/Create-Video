# MASTER VIDEO PRODUCTION PROMPT — NATURAL CYCLE

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Create an experimental cinematic short film titled **“NATURAL CYCLE”**, a surreal, monochromatic visual poem about death, decomposition, rebirth, and the endless cycle of nature.

- **Title:** NATURAL CYCLE
- **Genre:** Experimental short film, surrealism, dark art, visual poetry
- **Target duration:** 120 seconds (2 minutes, 3600 frames @ 30 FPS)
- **Preferred format:** Full HD 1920 × 1080, 16:9, 30 FPS
- **Color palette:** Strict black and white, pure solid white background (#FFFFFF) with black ink silhouette (#000000)
- **Visual style:** Minimalist, high contrast, organic silhouettes, dark surrealism, fine-art cinematography
- **Sound:** Atmospheric instrumental soundtrack (432Hz ambient drone) and carefully designed environmental sound effects
- **Audio Mode:** ambient-only (No dialogue, no voiceover narration)
- **Narrative structure:** Continuous transformation forming an endless visual loop

## 2. FACT-CHECK CONTRACT & CONSTRAINTS
- **Allowed Numbers:** ["120", "432", "4", "1", "2", "30", "75", "1080", "1920"]
- **Fruits Count:** Exactly 4 skull-shaped fruits on the mature tree branches.
- **Falling Fruit:** Exactly 1 skull fruit detaches and falls completely out of the frame before the transition.
- **Soil Line:** Locked permanently at 75% frame height (y = 810px) once established.
- **Camera during Disintegration:** Completely locked stationary.
- **Motion Principle:** Continuous fluid metamorphosis (anime / cinema style), NEVER a static 5-second Ken Burns slideshow.

## 3. STORYBOARD BEATS (120 SECONDS TOTAL)
1. **Title Sequence (0s - 10s):** Black screen transitions into elegant serif title "NATURAL CYCLE", subtle fade.
2. **The Skull Emerges (10s - 25s):** Intact feathered skull on pure white canvas, organic feathers breathing in wind.
3. **Cracking & Disintegration (25s - 50s):** Jagged zigzag fissures spread; fragments separate and fall downward under gravity like dark seeds.
4. **Soil Rises (50s - 65s):** Dark organic soil rises from bottom, covers fallen fragments, locks at 75% height.
5. **Seedling Emerges (65s - 80s):** A single sprout pushes through the soil, 2 feathered leaves unfold in continuous growth.
6. **Focus Zoom (80s - 90s):** Smooth cinematic push-in zoom centering on the delicate seedling.
7. **Tree Growth (90s - 105s):** Time-lapse botanical evolution; stem thickens into bone trunk, fractal branches expand.
8. **Four Skull Fruits (105s - 112s):** Exactly 4 skull-shaped fruits bud and mature across branches, swaying gently.
9. **Fruit Detachment & Fall (112s - 117s):** 1 fruit snaps and falls downward, exiting bottom edge completely.
10. **The Return & Final Card (117s - 120s):** Transition returns to original intact skull, completing the loop, followed by "THE END".
